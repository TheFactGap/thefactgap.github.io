Data Deletion

How to have your information removed from The Fact Gap's records

The only information we hold about viewers is the text, username and time of public comments on our videos. We delete it automatically after 90 days, and sooner if you ask.

To delete a comment we stored

  1. Email CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your username and the platform (YouTube, Instagram or TikTok). A link to the comment helps us find it faster.
  3. We delete every stored copy within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Deleting the comment on the platform itself removes it from public view; our stored copy is then removed at the next 90-day clean-up, or right away if you email us.

To disconnect The Fact Gap Publisher from an account

  • Google and YouTube: revoke access on the Google security settings page.
  • Instagram: remove the app in Instagram's settings for apps and websites.
  • TikTok: remove the app in TikTok's settings for connected apps.

Disconnecting stops all further access. To also delete what the Publisher already stored, email us as described above.
