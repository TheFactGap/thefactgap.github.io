Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information The Fact Gap collects, why, and how you can have it removed. It covers this website and The Fact Gap Publisher, the private tool we use to post our videos.

Who we are

The Fact Gap publishes short videos on YouTube (@thefactgap5), Instagram (@thefactgap) and TikTok (@thefactgap). You can reach us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

The Fact Gap Publisher runs on our own computer and connects only to The Fact Gap's own accounts. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

This website

  • It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or forms, and stores nothing on your device.
  • It loads no content from third parties; its fonts are hosted with the site.
  • It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

What The Fact Gap Publisher accesses

YouTube

The Fact Gap Publisher uses YouTube API Services. With our channel's permission it:

  • uploads our videos and sets their title, description, tags, captions, schedule and AI-content label;
  • reads our channel's videos and their statistics, such as views, watch time and audience retention;
  • reads public comments posted on our videos.

By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Instagram

With our professional account's permission, the Publisher posts our Reels and reads the insights (views, reach, watch time, likes, comments, shares and saves) and comments on our own posts.

TikTok

With our account's permission, the Publisher reads our basic profile information (display name and profile picture) to confirm it is posting to the right account, and sends our videos to our TikTok inbox as drafts, which we then publish ourselves.

Information about viewers

If you comment on one of our videos, the Publisher may read your public comment, your public username and the time you posted. We use this only to spot corrections and questions about our videos and to understand which topics people want more of. We do not collect anything else about viewers, and we never contact you outside the platform.

How information is stored and used

  • Everything the Publisher collects is stored on our own computer. Account sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's secure credential store; video details, statistics and comments are kept in a local database.
  • Comment text and usernames are deleted from our database after 90 days. Statistics about our own videos are kept for as long as the channel runs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • To write and check our videos and to summarise comment trends, we use Claude, an AI service made by Anthropic. Comment text may be processed by Anthropic for that purpose under its own terms and privacy policy, available in the Anthropic Legal Center.

Advertising and third-party content

This website shows no advertising and no third-party content. Our videos are hosted by YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, which may show advertising and collect information under their own policies.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google accounts: access granted to The Fact Gap Publisher can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Instagram and TikTok: access can be removed in each app's settings for connected apps and websites.
  • Your comments: delete a comment on the platform at any time. To have our stored copy deleted sooner than 90 days, follow the steps on our data deletion page. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

Our videos are for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Questions and complaints

Write to CONTACT_EMAIL with any privacy question or complaint. We reply within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
